Why we started this counter

I opened this centre in Phudkipur two years ago after seeing how far people had to travel just to submit one online form. Many of our neighbours lost a full day and their fare for a ten-minute job.

Here, I sit with you and do it step by step. If a document is missing, I tell you before you pay or travel again. If a service needs an authorised counter, I say so honestly.

Everyone who walks in is a neighbour first. That is the whole idea.
